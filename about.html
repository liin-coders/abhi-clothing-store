About Abhi Clothing Store

A neighbourhood menswear store where everyday wear, formals and wedding looks come together, with a tailor on hand for the perfect fit.

Visit our store

Menswear That Fits Your Life

Abhi Clothing Store is a neighbourhood menswear store in Narot Jaimal Singh. We bring everyday essentials, smart formals and wedding wear together, so you can find everything you need in a single visit.

Our team picks fabrics and fits with care, and our in-house tailor can stitch or alter any outfit, so what you wear always feels made for you.

  • Fabrics checked for quality and comfort
  • Custom tailoring and quick alterations
  • Honest prices and friendly advice
